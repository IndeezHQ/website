## 2. Personal Data We Collect

The data we collect depends on which features you use and how you interact with Indeez.

### A. Account and profile data

We may collect your name, email address, username, password or authentication credentials, birthdate or age confirmation, profile type, biography, profile image, pronouns, general location, social links, preferences, and other profile details.

For artist, label, venue, seller, or other professional accounts, we may also collect business name, role, authority, contact details, payment or tax-related details, roster or catalog information, and verification records.

### B. User content and music data

We collect content you upload or post, such as recordings, compositions, audio files, artwork, images, metadata, track and album details, credits, rights information, rights certifications, profiles, Top 5 selections, playlists, comments, replies, GIFs, links, event listings, and product listings.

Rights and verification records may include uploader identity, account ID, track identifier, ISRC if supplied, metadata, certification text and version, acceptance timestamp, claimed capacity, and related communications or evidence.

### C. Activity, discovery, and usage data

We may collect songs displayed, played, skipped, replayed, liked, saved, added to playlists or Top 5 lists, shared, hidden, or reported; searches; follows; profile and listing views; comments and reactions; feature usage; session dates and duration; navigation paths; and interactions with recommendations, advertising, editorial, event, and commerce features.

### D. Device, network, and technical data

We may collect IP address, device type, operating system, browser, app version, language, time zone, mobile or advertising identifiers where permitted, cookie or similar identifiers, referring pages, network information, crash logs, diagnostics, performance data, and security events.

We do not request access to precise location, contacts, microphone, camera, photos, Bluetooth, or push notifications unless a feature needs that permission. Your device will provide a permission request where required, and you can change permissions in device settings. Uploaded audio is content you choose to provide; it is not ambient microphone recording unless a clearly disclosed recording feature is offered and you enable it.

### E. Transactions and commerce data

If you buy, sell, subscribe, or use paid features, we and our payment, app-store, seller, fulfillment, shipping, tax, or fraud-prevention providers may process transaction amount, currency, purchase and subscription history, billing and shipping details, payment-method token or limited payment details, refund or chargeback records, tax information, and communications about the transaction.

Indeez may not receive your complete payment-card number when a payment processor or app store handles payment.

### F. Communications and support data

We collect messages you send to us, support requests, survey responses, reports, appeals, rights complaints, safety communications, and related attachments. We may record the date, participants, status, and outcome of a support or moderation matter.

### G. Identity, rights, safety, and fraud data

Where appropriate, we may collect identity or authority documentation, payment-method verification, signals of duplicate or unauthorized uploads, device and account relationships, suspected fraud indicators, moderation history, blocked-user relationships, complaint records, and evidence needed to protect users or rights holders.

### H. Information from other sources

We may receive personal data from:

- other users, such as when they tag, report, credit, or communicate about you;
- artists, labels, venues, sellers, distributors, representatives, and rights holders;
- payment processors, app stores, fulfillment providers, shipping providers, and transaction partners;
- authentication, identity-verification, fraud-prevention, moderation, security, analytics, hosting, and support providers;
- publicly available sources when needed to verify identity, professional claims, events, rights, or safety; and
- business partners when you direct or authorize the disclosure.
