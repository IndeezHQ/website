## Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Indeez account and its data at any time, directly in the app:

1. Open the Indeez app and sign in.
2. Tap your profile menu (top-right).
3. Scroll to "Delete account" and confirm.

**What is deleted:** your account and all associated data — your posts, uploaded songs, albums and playlists, events, followers and follows, messages, and liked songs. If you are the sole owner/admin of any artist, label, venue, or store page, those pages and their content are deleted too.

**What is kept:** we may retain limited records where required by law (e.g. for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes) for a limited period, after which they are deleted.

If you need help deleting your account, contact [support@indeez.world](mailto:support@indeez.world).
